Everything you need to stay on track with your health

Most reminder apps fail because phone batteries put them to sleep or notifications get lost. MediBell is built from the ground up as a clinical-grade alarm system.

🔔

Full-Screen Lock Alarm

When medicine time arrives, MediBell wakes your phone with a full-screen alarm, showing medicine name, dosage, and food instructions.

  • Rings even when phone is locked or muted
  • Take, Snooze or Skip directly from lock screen
  • Voice announcement in English or Hindi
🗓️

Flexible Medical Schedules

Handles complex doctor prescriptions easily: daily, alternating days, specific weekdays, or multi-week cycles (e.g., 21 days on, 7 days off).

  • Multiple times a day with meal tags
  • SOS / As-Needed medicines with minimum safety gap
  • Course durations (e.g. 5 days of Antibiotics)
📦

Pill Stock & Refill Alerts

Every time you mark a dose as taken, MediBell automatically decrements your stock count and alerts you days before you run out.

  • Calculates remaining days of supply
  • Customizable low-stock notification threshold
  • Refill button with quick quantity reload
🩺

Health Vitals & Doctor Reports

Log daily vitals like Blood Pressure, Pulse, Blood Sugar, and Body Weight. View beautiful adherence trends and export printable PDFs.

  • Weekly and monthly adherence percentage
  • One-click PDF medical report for your doctor
  • Smart miss patterns (e.g. missed night doses)
👓

Elder Friendly & High Contrast

Tailored specifically for older adults and family members who struggle with tiny fonts or complicated menus.

  • Elder Mode: Jumbo text and oversized touch buttons
  • Spoken voice reminders in Hindi and English
  • Full Dark Mode for comfortable night viewing
🔒

100% Privacy & Keystore Security

No user account, no login, no cloud database. Your sensitive health data never leaves your device and is protected by hardware encryption.

  • No INTERNET permission requested in Android Manifest
  • Encrypted with Android Keystore key
  • Biometric Fingerprint / Face App Lock

Yes! MediBell utilizes Android's USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permissions. When an alarm time arrives, it wakes up the screen and rings like an alarm clock, allowing you to take, snooze, or skip the dose without unlocking your device. Privacy policies are promises, but lack of internet permission is a mathematical guarantee. By not including android.permission.INTERNET in the app's manifest, Android OS physically prevents MediBell from sending any data over Wi-Fi or cellular networks.
